What I look forward to most

Not just the wedding.
The life after it.

The ordinary mornings. The little jokes only we understand. Asking how your day went. Sharing meals, making time for each other, praying together, and learning how to handle difficult days with mercy.

I want the beautiful moments, yes. But I want to learn how to honour you through the ordinary ones, too.

A promise to keep working on myself
✧

I want to become
a good husband.

Not a man who promises perfection, but a man who keeps learning.

To listen before reacting. To speak with kindness. To take responsibility. To respect your feelings. To admit when I'm wrong. To encourage your growth and protect the trust we build together.

May my love for you be seen in how I treat you, not only in the words I say.

A prayer for our future

Ya Allah,
make it easy for us.

If this marriage is good for our deen, our lives, and our hereafter, bring it closer to us in a way that pleases You.

Bless our efforts. Guide our decisions. Help us prepare wisely. Make us patient with the process and grateful for every step You make possible.

We plan · We prepare · We trust Allah
